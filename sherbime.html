Our Services

Trust Music™ — Marc Hill (Producer & DJ) offers a full range of professional audio services for every music project.

🎙️

Professional Recording

Our studio offers professionally treated acoustic space for recording vocals, instruments and full bands. We use top-tier microphones (Neumann TLM 102) and premium preamps to capture every detail of your performance.

  • Isolated acoustic booth
  • Professional microphones (Neumann TLM 102)
  • Premium preamps and converters
  • Real-time monitoring for the artist
🎚️

Mix & Master

Our mixing engineers use modern and analogue techniques to create a clean, dynamic, radio-ready sound. Final mastering ensures your song sounds equally powerful on every device — from phones to clubs.

  • Full mix with up to 48 channels
  • Dynamic processing and precise EQ
  • Spatial effects (reverb, delay)
  • International-standard mastering
  • Delivery in WAV, MP3, FLAC
🎹

Music Production

Bring your idea and we'll turn it into a complete song. Our producers create beats, arrangements and original productions for every genre — pop, hip-hop, R&B, folk, electronic and more. We have a rich sound library and professional synthesizers.

  • Beat and arrangement creation
  • Drum programming and virtual instruments
  • Sound design and electronic textures
  • Tailored to any music genre
  • Close collaboration with the artist throughout the process
🎧

Audio Consulting

Not sure about the direction of your project? Our team offers professional consulting to help with song structure, arrangement, sound selection and every other technical and artistic aspect. A professional eye (and ear) can make the difference between a good song and a hit.

  • Song structure analysis
  • Arrangement and production advice
  • Sound and texture selection
  • Detailed feedback on your existing mix
  • One-hour consultation sessions
